Our History

From one family kitchen to ten branches

Tisay Dining began with a simple idea: serve honest Filipino food the way it is cooked at home, and make every guest feel like family.

It all started in Himamaylan City, Negros Occidental, in a small kitchen where recipes handed down through generations were cooked for neighbors, relatives and friends. A few tables, a handful of classic dishes and a lot of heart were all it took to fill the room every evening.

Word spread from one table to the next. Diners came back for the slow-simmered sinigang, the crisp lechon kawali and the warm welcome that came with every plate. Desserts and refreshing drinks soon joined the menu, turning a simple meal into an occasion.

Today, Tisay Dining keeps the same promise in every city we serve: fresh ingredients, familiar flavors and a modern table where everyone is welcome. Our growth has never changed what matters most, which is the food and the people who share it.

  1. 2010

    The first table

    Our doors open in Himamaylan City with a short menu of family recipes.

  2. 2014

    A growing menu

    Desserts and signature drinks join the menu, and the dining room doubles in size.

  3. 2018

    Across the Visayas

    New branches in Iloilo City and Cebu City bring Tisay Dining to more island neighbors.

  4. 2022

    Nationwide

    Branches open in Luzon and Mindanao, bringing us to ten cities across the country.
